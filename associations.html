Associations & Collaborations

Fr. Roby's far-reaching network of partnerships with international organizations, institutions, and leaders united for peace.

Global Network

Connected With The World For Peace

Over decades of service, Fr. Roby has built meaningful relationships with institutions, national governments, religious bodies, and civil society organizations across the globe — all united by the shared vision of a more harmonious world.

United Nations

NGO Representative at the United Nations

Fr. Roby serves as an official NGO Representative at the United Nations, participating in sessions in New York, Geneva, and Vienna. In this capacity, he advocates for human rights, interfaith harmony, sustainable development, and the rights of marginalized communities.

His participation at the UN allows him to bring the voices of India's diverse communities to the global stage, ensuring that the perspective of civil society is heard in international decision-making.
